Geometry in Eyes of Spinoza

Spinoza structured his Ethics like a geometric proof — Definitions, Axioms, Propositions, Proofs. He believed geometry offered "the most objective path to understanding," and that natural laws flow from God's essence the same way triangular properties follow from their definition.

Karl Jaspers and the Axial Age

Between 800 and 200 BCE, five civilizations — China, India, Persia, Greece, and the Hebrew tradition — independently crossed the same threshold of consciousness. Jaspers called it the Axial Age: the simultaneous birth of critical self-awareness, universal ethics, and transcendent thought, without any contact between them.
