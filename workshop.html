01 · The word

What is an Agalma?

Every workshop ends with one. It's worth knowing what the word means before you build one.
ἄ
NOUN · ANCIENT GREEK
Agalma (ἄγαλμα)
A sacred or treasured object, created with care and devotion. In Mirbreak, the agalma is your final form — a small mirror-tiled body whose surface quirks mask deeper insights. Each face captures a triangular fragment of the room, of the light, of you.
