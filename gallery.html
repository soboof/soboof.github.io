Geometric
abstract
figures. All different · all relative.

A sacred geometry that governs growth. Through it, each fragment — though uniform in law — emerges in organic singularity. Trees grow by rules, yet no two leaves are the same. Crystals form by symmetry, yet every shard reflects a unique glint of the whole.

Each piece is a fragment of architecture, a shard of cosmos, a reflection of the self. Built with Mirbreak modular blocks and hand-cut mirror tiles using Ayeneh-Kari technique — these sculptures transform light into living geometry.
