SOBOOF · CREATIEVE WORKSHOP · NL

Make your own
mirror sculpture. A two-level Ayeneh-Kari workshop.

Over a two-level creatieve workshop, learn the ancient Persian craft of Ayeneh-Kari (mirror mosaic) and apply it to both a traditional framed mirror and a modern sculptural form built from MirBreak modular blocks.

Leave with two creations — a wall-hanging mirror and your own mirror-tiled sculpture. No skills needed.

01 · What you'll do

Two levels,
two things to take home.

Take the levels separately or together. Day one is the technique. Day two is the sculpture.
DAY 01 · STARTER £ 45
Hanging mirror.
Make a 15×20 cm framed mirror — hands-on Persian mirror-cutting from zero.
OUTPUT · 15×20 CM FRAMED MIRROR
01
Hands-on Persian mirror-cutting techniques — score, snap, shape.
02
Make a 15 × 20 cm framed hanging mirror to take home.
03
Introduction to geometric patterns and the history of Ayeneh-Kari.
04
Explore 3D shape-making with MirBreak blocks — experiment with shapes you'd like to tile next.
Take home · 1× framed mirror, 15×20 cm
DAY 02 · ADVANCED £ 85
Mirrored sculpture.
Build and tile a full 3D Mirbreak sculpture — your own modular relic.
OUTPUT · 3D MIRRORED SCULPTURE
01
Build the base of your sculpture using interlocking modular blocks.
02
Cut triangular mirror tiles and begin applying them to your 3D base — face by face.
03
Take home your trophy: a one-of-a-kind mirrored sculpture.
Take home · 1× mirrored sculpture
